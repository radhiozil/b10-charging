Battery & charging efficiency

Battery capacity turns battery percentages into kWh. The efficiency for each charger type is used to estimate a missing "before" or "after" %. Start with the typical values, log both percentages for a few sessions per charger type, then tap Use measured averages.

About

Your records live in your own Google-signed account and are not visible to other users. Use Export CSV on the Records tab for a backup.
